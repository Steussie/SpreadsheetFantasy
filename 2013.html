2013 Spreadsheet Fantasy Year in Review

After three years of good and bad, 2013 was the year that Brent finally put it all together. Adam Scott can be thanked for Brent's blowout of the rest of the league, as the Aussie won the Masters and finished in the top five of the Open and PGA Championships. Brent's love-hate relationship with Zach Johnson was all love in 2013 as he scored 50 points in four consecutive weeks on his way to a 545 point season. 2013 would be Dave's last season, as he seemed to lose interest and would be replaced by Daniel the following year. Although he missed the podium, Beast continued to churn out top tens, thanks to Matt Kuchar, Jim Furyk, and the relatively unknown (at the time), Jason Day. Lastly, Tiger Woods had his final 'Tiger-like' Season with 5 wins and nearly 1000 points, though Bryce's depth let him down once again as the rest of his team apart from Bill Haas, failed to perform.
