2014 Spreadsheet Fantasy Year in Review

After a 2013 season which saw Brent dismantle the rest of the league with the help of Adam Scott, 2014 was the year when James reclaimed his spot at the top of the standings. An Abysmal start to the year was remedied when Rory McIlroy rattled off three straight victories, including the Open Championship and PGA Championship. James' 655 point week in the PGA Championship shattered the previous one-week record and was key in overtaking Beast at the top of the Standings. Entering the final week with just a 65 point lead over the aforementioned Beast, James held on thanks to a konkeled 2nd place finish from McIlroy. With the final margin of victory being just 50 points, 2014 was as down to the wire as they come.
