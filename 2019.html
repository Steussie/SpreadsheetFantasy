2019 Spreadsheet Fantasy Year in Review

Brent is back on top! It was six long years ago since Brent won his first SSF Championship. While James made a charge in the final few weeks, the astronomical lead Brent established after his Konkeled victories at both the Masters and U.S. Open was just too much to overcome. Brooks Koepka was the runaway MVP of Brent's squad, racking up more than 450 points in the four Majors alone! Resurgences from Tiger Woods, Ian Poulter, and Brandt Snedeker helped round out a team of players eager to prove that glory hasn't quite passed them by. In second place, James finished the season just 85 points back, largely carried by his big three of Rory McIlroy, Jon Rahm, and Xander Schauffele. Bryce rounds out the podium, following up his back-to-back victories with a third-place finish. The ever-consistent Dustin Johnson did his thing again this year, and was joined by Patrick Cantlay, who had a true breakout year. Congratulations to Brent, your 2019 Spreadsheet Fantasy Champion!
