2021 Spreadsheet Fantasy Year in Review

For the first time in Spreadsheet Fantasy's 12 year history, someone other than Beast, Bryce, Brent or James will sit atop the SSF podium. In his first full SSF season, Jon Mark showed up big time in the big events, racking up nearly 2500 points in Tier 3 and 4 events. After finding himself in seventh place through the first 13 weeks of the season, Jon's team turned it on at the Masters, carding 280 points. He didn't stop there, rolling to an impressive 31 top tens and more than 3000 points in the final 22 weeks. By the time the Olympics rolled around Jon was less than 100 points out of first place, and after a monster 630 point finish, he grabbed the lead and never gave it back. Congratulations to Spreadsheet Fantasy's 2021 Champion, Jon Mark!
