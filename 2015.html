2015 Spreadsheet Fantasy Year in Review

2015 was all Beast. From start to finish, Beast shattered every record in the book enroute to a 1500 point blowout. The perfect storm of Jordan Spieth and Jason Day scored nearly 2400 points by themselves, and were helped out by team veterans Kuchar and Furyk. Beast's 11 wins and 47 top tens are both new records, as are the 3 Major Championships he captured. While it was Beast's season to lose, both Daniel and James put together solid performances which, in other years may have been good enough to win. With Fuchs' departure after the 2014, his team was taken over by Jarret, who had a bit of a rough first season, while Lawrence narrowly avoided his third last place finish in four years.
