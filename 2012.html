2012 Spreadsheet Fantasy Year in Review

As difficult a feat as there is in sports, James captured the Spreadsheet Fantasy Three-peat in 2012, setting a new record for wins with nine, and points with 3690. With Dave being his closest competition nearly 400 points back, and the next best nearly 1000, James truly dominated the 2012 season. Rory McIlroy won four times, while Dufner waggled his way to a pair of victories and 590 points. Waiting on Tiger finally paid off for Bryce in 2012, as he won three times and finished with the second most points, earning Bryce his second third place finish. 2012 gave us a new addition to the league in Lawrence, whose feuds with Fuchs would provide endless entertainment and love of Miguel Angel Jimenez was unparalleled.
