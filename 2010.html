2010 Spreadsheet Fantasy Year in Review

2010 was the first year of the Spreadsheet Fantasy golf league. Beginning a few weeks after the start of the PGA Tour year, the shortened season was just 32 weeks, but saw Cody set a record of 7 wins in his first year. Sadly, 2010 can also be viewed as the last time Phil Mickelson was truly a powerhouse in the fantasy golf world, as he captured his third Masters title and a second place finish at the U.S. Open. 2010 was also the year when 10th round pick Matt Kuchar announced himself as a cornerstone of Beast's team for years to come. In the end, James captured the first ever Richard J. Osicki Memorial Trophy with a 90 point victory over Beast, beginning their heavyweight rivalry.
