2016 Spreadsheet Fantasy Year in Review

2016 marked the second year in a row that Spreadsheet Fantasy had a run away winner. Despite a rocky start to the season, James shattered the previous FedEx Cup points record on his way to a nearly 800 point win over Beast. Recording an unbelievable 1250 points and 16 top tens in the final four weeks, James claimed his fifth Spreadsheet Fantasy title in epic fashion. 2016 also marked another first, as Mrs. Beast joined the gang, bringing the league up to eight managers for the first time. Player of the year honours for 2016 go to Dustin Johnson, who carded all three of Bryce's wins, and a league leading 1050 points.
