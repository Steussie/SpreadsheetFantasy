2011 Spreadsheet Fantasy Year in Review

Following up the inaugural season, 2011 again proved to be James' year, as the defending champ rode the hot hands of Nick Watney and Webb Simpson to a seven win season and set a new top tens record of 44. The Immergence of Brent after a disappointing debut in 2010 also garnered national headlines, with Luke Donald leading the league in points, and netting Brent a runner-up finish. Meanwhile, Bryce went the opposite way, as Tiger struggled to regain his form. Beast quietly scored his second consecutive podium finish, a place he would frequent in the coming years. 2011 was also the first year our dear friend Fuchs would tee it up as part of the league, inviting his friend Dave who would spend three seasons in the league.
