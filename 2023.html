2023 Spreadsheet Fantasy Year in Review

Another great year of Spreadsheet Fantasy, With team Kevan (Kristen and Evan) taking home the gold. They had a close call in their inaugural 2022 season, leading deep into the year before dropping to third, but there was no such falter in 2023. From the Masters onward they held onto first place, thanks to an incredible 1200 point season from Scottie Scheffler. Sung-Jae Im also had another great year, while Tommy Fleetwood turned the jets on down the stretch to keep them on top. Their 47 top tens has them tied for second all-time, with four of their five wins coming in elevated events. Congrats to our 2023 champions, Team Kevan!
