2018 Spreadsheet Fantasy Year in Review

They say lightning never strikes twice, but apparently no one told Bryce that. For the first time since James won his third straight Championship in 2012, SSF has a back to back winner. On the backs of 2018's top two scorers, Bryce once again took it to the rest of the league, racking up a massive 48 top tens to go with his 8 wins. Bryce's start to the season was average, sticking around the middle of the pack before stepping on the gas in week 22. In the final 16 weeks Bryce racked up 28 top tens to go with five wins, including three in a row starting with the Open Championship. He also never seemed to have an off week, smashing the record for most 100+ point weeks with 20. He'll almost certainly be keeping his elite pairing of Dustin Johnson and Justin Thomas, and will be one of the favorites to make it three in a row in 2019.
