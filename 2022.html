2022 Spreadsheet Fantasy Year in Review

It took nine long seasons, but Daniel finally took home his first Spreadsheet Fantasy Championship in 2022. Wins in four of the final six weeks catapulted Daniel into first place, and a pair of top tens in the season finale was enough to hang on for a 25 point victory over James. Will Zalatoris was the breakout star of the season, racking up 780 points in just 19 starts, while Patrick Cantlay set a new career high with 895. Daniel's personal favorite Tony Finau also overcame some early season struggles, winning in back to back weeks to salvage a very solid year. Daniel has always been near the top of the leaderboard in top tens, and this year was no different. Thanks to his league-leading 44 top tens he's been the third fastest manager to hit the 300 mark. After a winless 2020, he now has 12 wins over the last couple of seasons, and seems poised to contend for years to come. Congratulations to our 2022 Spreadsheet Fantasy Champion, Daniel!
