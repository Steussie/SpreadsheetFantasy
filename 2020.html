2020 Spreadsheet Fantasy Year in Review

What a season! If I would have told you a year ago, that Bryce would score 660 points at the Masters, in the final week of the season, in the middle of November, making up a 200 point deficit and passing two other teams, to win a season of 31 weeks stretched out over 11 months, you’d probably think I’d been smoking some of the good stuff with our boy Fuchs. Nevertheless, that’s exactly what happened in 2020. Undoubtably the weirdest and probably the most compelling season on record, 2020 had a bit of everything. A two month hiatus in the middle of the year, an improbable comeback, a Konkeled Major win in the final week of the season, multiple other rivalries coming down to the final tournament, (and lets not forget mega-Bryson and his undressing of Winged Foot), it’s been a year to remember.
